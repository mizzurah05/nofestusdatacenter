Why Showing Up Matters

Backroom deals happen when leadership thinks nobody is watching. Packed rooms, standing-room-only crowds, and relentless public comments are the exact tools that put national pressure on officials and forced accountability.

Keep the Pressure On

Even after historic municipal election upsets, remaining officials and corporate interests count on community fatigue. Showing up to meetings proves that residents are paying close attention to every vote, resolution, and closed-door discussion.
